Our Story

From Classmates to Best Friends ❤️

Slide 1 of 4

♡
2015–2018 | Just Classmates

We were classmates at school, but barely interacted. Maybe a word or two while rehearsing a play for our school’s alumni meet, but nothing more than that.

♡
September 2021 | The First “Hii”

A simple “Hii” on Instagram. Neither of us knew that this little interaction would eventually lead to such a meaningful bond.

♡
A Long Gap | Life Went On

After that, we drifted apart again, with no regular conversations.

♡
January 2023 | The Second Interaction

We started talking again, and this time, things slowly picked up. Long phone calls and late-night video calls became a part of our routine. Conversations started lasting longer, and we began getting to know each other better.

♡
After May 2023 | Another Long Gap

Life took its own course. I got into a relationship, and our conversations became occasional — a few chats and phone calls here and there, with long gaps in between.

♡
April 2024 | Finding Our Way Back

After my breakup, we started having proper conversations again. Slowly, the connection we had once shared began finding its way back into our lives, and this time, it continued.

♡
February 2025 | A Birthday to Remember

On her birthday, we had a long video call, and somehow, the calls continued throughout the day. It was one of those moments when talking didn’t feel like an effort; it just felt natural.

♡
April 2025 | Our First Meeting

We finally met in person for the first time. Our bond grew stronger, and we started understanding each other on a deeper level. There were a few little ups and downs, as there are in any bond, but nothing major — just small moments that we worked through.

♡
September 2025 | A Whole Night on Call

We spent the entire night on a call, and something about our dynamic shifted a little. The comfort grew, the conversations felt even more natural, and our bond became stronger than before.

♡
October 2025 | When Things Felt Different

We met again, and this time, something genuinely changed. We got much closer, spent time visiting different places, shared countless funny moments, and created some really cute memories together. Being around each other started feeling even more special, effortless, and comfortable.

♡
Since Then | No Looking Back

And since then, there’s been no looking back. We have become the best-friends duo who understand each other, share the silliest moments, have meaningful conversations, and feel completely comfortable being ourselves around each other.

Somehow, after all the gaps, missed chances to talk, and unexpected reunions, we’ve found our way into each other’s lives in a way that feels right.

From school classmates to the kind of best friends who feel like a safe place — it’s been quite a journey. 🤍
